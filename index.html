How We Work

Operators,
not advisors.

Most consultants hand you a slide deck and disappear. We stay, build, and operate. Every engagement ends with working software — not a playbook collecting dust.

Step 01

Discovery & Audit

We spend the first week understanding your workflows, tools, team structure, and pain points. No templates — every engagement starts with listening.

Step 02

Strategy & Scope

A clear path: what to build, where AI applies, what to skip. We agree on a tight scope before writing a line of code.

Step 03

Build & Iterate

Fast sprints, real feedback loops. We ship working software weekly and adjust based on what your team actually does with it.

Step 04

Operate & Improve

We stay on to run the systems we build. Monitoring, improvements, new features — tools don't go stale when the project closes.
